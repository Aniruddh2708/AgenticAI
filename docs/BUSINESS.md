# VibeFinance — commercial concept slide

**Problem:** Retail investors need evidence that connects market signals to their own
limits, horizon and behaviour, rather than another generic market feed.

**Product:** A cited research dashboard with transparent signals, disagreement
explanations, portfolio context and relevant watchlist alerts. This prototype uses
synthetic data and makes no claim of actual investment performance.

| Tier | Customer value | Revenue concept |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Basic watchlist, source inspection, educational signals | Acquisition and trust |
| Personalized | Profile comparison, behaviour nudges, what-if and history | Subscription research tools |
| B2B | Typed research API and auditable evidence for fintechs/advisors | Usage-based or licensed API |

**Before production:** Licensed data and authentic disclosures; point-in-time
evaluation; audited confidence; authentication and privacy controls; advisor
partnerships and appropriate SEBI compliance. No pricing, returns or regulatory
status are asserted by this demo.
